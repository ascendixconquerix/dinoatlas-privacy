Dino Atlas: Dinosaur Guide — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · Effective immediately

Data controller: Ascendix Conquerix — contact sarahhhaa0102@gmail.com.

Dino Atlas: Dinosaur Guide ("the App", package com.ascendixconquerix.dinoatlas) is developed by Ascendix Conquerix. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and never asks for your name, email, phone number, or precise location. All dinosaur content (480 species, images, and the guessing game) is bundled inside the App and works fully offline. However, the App integrates the following third-party services, which may automatically collect certain data:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and general device information (model, OS version, language, screen size) to serve and measure ads. Ads are limited to the "G" (general audiences) content rating. See Google's Advertising Policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) (consent management) — shows a consent form to users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland and stores your consent choice on your device so it can be honored by the advertising SDK.

We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and we do not operate our own servers that receive data from the App.

2. Locally Stored Data

Your favorite dinosaurs, your chosen language, your coin balance and progress in the guessing game, and a small counter used to space out interstitial ads are stored only on your device using Android app preferences. This data is never uploaded by us and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data in Android Settings.

When you use the Share feature, the App temporarily copies the selected species image to its private cache folder so it can be handed to the app you choose (messaging, email, social media, etc.). Whatever you share goes directly to that app under its own privacy policy; we do not receive it.

3. Permissions

  • Internet & network state — required only to load ads and the consent form. The App's own content does not need a connection.

The App does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, or notifications.

4. Advertising, Consent & Your Choices

Ads are served by Google AdMob. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent (via a Google-certified consent form) before any personalized advertising data is used. If you decline, the App does not request personalized ads.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time: open the App, tap the language/settings button and choose Ad privacy settings (shown when consent is required in your region). You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

5. Data Sharing & Retention

We do not sell your data. Data collected by the Google services listed above is processed under Google's own privacy policies and retention periods. We have no access to any data that identifies you personally.

6. Your Rights (EEA / UK)

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Data stored by the App lives only on your device, so you can access or delete it yourself by using the App or by clearing its data or uninstalling it. Data collected by the advertising provider is held by Google; if you would like us to help forward a request to them, or have any question about your data, contact us at sarahhhaa0102@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a dinosaur encyclopedia suitable for curious kids as well as adult dinosaur fans. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Ads shown in the App are restricted to the "G" (general audiences) content rating, and no account, name, or contact details are ever requested. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, contact us at sarahhhaa0102@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the new version at this URL.

9. Contact
